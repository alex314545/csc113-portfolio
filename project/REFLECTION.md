Reflection Questions
What was your biggest "aha moment" when working with AI this semester?

Building the website from scratch by using AI and using various prompts to customize the AI's responses. It was really surprising for me to see and satisfying to figure out.

Describe a time when the AI gave you something useless. What did you do next?

When I was doing the first iterations for my final project, lots of the functions didn't work. However, I kept customizing the prompts and focusing on one thing at a time.

How did your prompting approach change from Week 1 to now?

I used to try to do it in one go, but I realized the responses would include mistakes or errors. Now, I focus on asking one thing at a time and adding to it slowly.

What's one thing you can do with AI now that you couldn't do in August?

Building websites and programs

What surprised you most about working with AI tools?

The capabilities and how customizable the AI is with the use of prompts.

What's something AI is genuinely bad at that you discovered firsthand?

Trying to do a lot of things at once. By trying to do everything in one prompt will not lead to accurate responses.

How might you use AI tools in your other classes or your job?

When I'm confused or unfamiliar with a topic, asking for help and explanations.

What would you tell a friend who's never used AI tools to help them get started?

I would tell them to start slow, focusing on asking questions and adding one at a time. Being comfortable to find a prompt that best fits the situation.

Is there anything you want to learn more about after this course?

Yes, I want to learn more in depth on the function of AI prompts and the way AI adapts to them.

On a scale of 1-10, how confident do you feel working with AI tools now versus when the semester started? Why?

I would say an eight, I'm definitely more confident but I still have much to learn. I feel like I'm just scratching the surface of AI and I'm excited to continue researching and discovering more.
