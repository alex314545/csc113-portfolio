# UNIBOT Improvement Report

## The Single Change
"I changed the explanation from it just changing the terms to actually put what word it's referring to beside it"

## Why THIS Change?
It was the main cause for the explanation being confusing. Adding this one sentence clears the explanation up immensely and I actually know what term the AI is referring to.

- **After:** The AI now tells me what word it replaced right beside it.

## Unintended Consequences
It's more clutter since the AI has to now put which term it's referring to right beside the unicorn term.

## Still Broken (On Purpose)
1. The unicorn terms - I felt like that would've destroyed what the main personality of what the AI was.
2. The constant emojis - It adds character and personality to the responses it gives.
3. "Changes" wording to fit unicorn terms - It's funny to see how the AI could structure a sentence when trying to fit unicorn terms into it.

## Lesson Learned
Just adding one sentence can really help to adjust the AI's responses in case I need a clearer explanation or just didn't like how it did something.
